EnTIQ
Commercial Module Blueprint
Product, administration and development specification for the complete module catalogue
Version 1.1  |  1 August 2026
BLUEPRINT PURPOSEDefine what each EnTIQ module looks like, how users move through it, which data it owns, and how all modules work together.
1. Blueprint principles
One EnTIQ account, one client and entity graph, one permissions model and one audit standard.
Each module can be purchased separately but must work immediately with every other enabled module.
Practice HQ is the only practice administration surface for subscriptions, integrations, settings, users and access.
Control Centre is restricted to EnTIQ operators and controls practices, platform operations, security and support.
Client information, identity checks, documents and relationships are captured once and reused with permission.
Every important decision keeps its source evidence, policy version, human owner and audit chronology.
AI detects, explains and drafts. High-risk decisions, advice, signatures, credit and money movement remain controlled.
Brickbanq, Recover and Restructure are retired products. Useful components are reused inside active modules.
2. Shared product experience
Navigation: A consistent left module switcher, contextual workspace navigation, global search, notifications and user menu.
Home: A role-based dashboard showing work due, risks, approvals, recent activity and next best actions.
Workspaces: Clients, entities, cases, jobs, projects, funds and settlements use a common workspace pattern.
Queues: Review, approval, exception and escalation queues use consistent status, owner, priority and SLA controls.
Client experience: EnTIQ Client presents only enabled modules and permitted actions in plain English.
Accessibility: Responsive layouts, keyboard support, clear focus, readable contrast and screen-reader labels are release requirements.
